Framework 01 · Ranking

Velocity-Driven Ranking Loop

OTAs reward freshness. Stale listings decay quietly.
Decision rule: many small bookings beat few high-margin nights.
Trigger: recent-bookings-count drops below the 7-day baseline.
Action: discount-stack toggle, not a base-price cut.
Velocity is leading. Price is lagging.

Framework 04 · Benchmark

Benchmark Parity

Framework 08 · Hierarchy

Lever Hierarchy under Demand Softness

1

Reduce blackout coverage (96N to 59N)

Expands available inventory at a controlled discount tier.

2

Reduce MinStay (2 to 1)

Activates a new arrival segment without touching base price.

3

Cut base price (last resort)

Direct margin hit. Use only after lever 1 and 2 are exhausted.

Lever 1 + 2 expand inventory. Lever 3 shrinks margin.

Live Pipeline · Part 01

Apollo Cache Extraction

OTA backend = GraphQL behind a logged-in PMS-Web.
Bearer-token captured via a fetch-interceptor in the browser console.
Schema discovered via error-pattern probing. Introspection is disabled.

Live Pipeline · Part 03

Change-Log + Recheck Cadence

Every tuning logged: before / after, occupancy snapshot, cluster overview.
