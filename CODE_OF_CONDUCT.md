# Code of Conduct

The Cape Town AI Exchange runs on trust. There is no moderation team — we keep it good by being good contributors.

## Be useful

Show up with something concrete. A working demo, a real opinion, a question you tried to answer first. "What do you think about AI?" is not a contribution.

## Be honest

Say what worked AND what did not. Promote what you genuinely use. Do not promote what you only built to sell. If you have a financial interest, disclose it.

## Respect the room

- People's time is the scarcest resource. Cap demos at the agreed length.
- One person speaks at a time.
- Pronoun preferences are explicit, not guessed at.
- "I do not know" is a complete sentence.

## Respect client confidentiality

Many of us run advisory or operator businesses. Do not name a client, project, or
individual without their consent. Aggregated patterns and anonymized examples are
fine and useful.

## What gets you removed from the crew

- Sharing private session content publicly without consent
- Plagiarising another crew member's work
- Repeated harassment after being asked to stop
- Using the crew as a free lead pipeline

These are not gradations — they are removal triggers. The maintainer makes the call
and explains it briefly.

## How to raise concerns

If something feels off, message [chris@erlerventures.org](mailto:chris@erlerventures.org)
directly. Concerns are confidential. The maintainer will get back to you within a week.

This is a small community. We can keep it good with small, deliberate effort.
